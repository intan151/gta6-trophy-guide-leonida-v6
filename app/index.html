    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official trophy descriptions alone. Five stages, in order — story first, then side missions, collectibles, activities, and the cleanup feats last. The platinum pops when the other 54 are earned.

      How the stages map to the trophies

      Each stage lists the trophies it covers. Earned trophies show checked, so the roadmap doubles as a progress check.

      Missable trophies — grab them on your first pass

      These 19 trophies are tied to a specific story mission, a one-time moment, or a choice you cannot repeat cleanly. The named story missions can be replayed from the mission list, but the moment trophies are easiest on the first pass — do them when they come up, not later.

        About this guide

        Names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation 5 list, as published by Exophase and PSNProfiles on 2026-11-18. Every guide is written in our own words for this entry and cites its sources beside each trophy: the official list for the facts, and our own advice built from the official description — not yet checked in the game — for the steps. Specific locations are only named where the official description names them; where the game has not pinned a place down yet, the guide says so instead of guessing.
